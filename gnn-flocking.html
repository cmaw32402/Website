GNN for Flocking Control

Training a graph neural network to imitate a classical multi-robot flocking controller, then comparing the two in a six-robot Webots simulation.

Approach

Six E-puck robots followed a square waypoint route through obstacle passages. A classical Laplacian-based controller handled formation maintenance, navigation, and obstacle avoidance.

Robot states and expert commands were collected as graph training samples. A message-passing GNN learned to predict each robot's two-dimensional control vector and was tested in the same Webots arena.

Results

The GNN produced similar formation error and completion time to the classical controller, but obstacle avoidance was less reliable: the learned controller had three collisions.

Metric Classical GNN
Average formation error 0.012 0.013
Peak formation error 0.092 0.092
Cycle completion time 488 s 493 s
Collisions 0 3

The model was trained on approximately 4,000 samples from one arena and repeated route, so performance on different environments or robot counts remains untested.
